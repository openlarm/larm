## Why a CCLA

LARM is licensed under [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). Most
contributions arrive from individuals who certify each commit with a
DCO sign-off (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`). Some contributions arrive from
employees acting on behalf of an employer. In that case the employer,
not the employee, owns the copyright in most jurisdictions, so the
employer needs to grant the necessary licences to the project once,
covering all current and future employees.

A signed CCLA gives the project clear legal standing to accept those
contributions and gives the employer clear assurance that its
employees' contributions are authorised.

## Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

- **"You" (or "Your")** means the corporate entity that is making this
  Agreement with the LARM project.
- **"Project"** means the LARM open-source project, currently
  maintained at https://github.com/openlarm (URL active once the org
  is registered).
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including
  any modifications or additions to existing work, that is
  intentionally submitted by You or by anyone authorised by You to the
  Project for inclusion in, or documentation of, any of the products
  owned or managed by the Project.
- **"Designated Employees"** means the individuals You authorise to
  submit Contributions on Your behalf, listed in Schedule A below or in
  any subsequent written supplement.

- The originality or licensability of any previously submitted

## How to sign

1. **Read this Agreement carefully**, ideally with legal counsel.
2. Print and sign the **Signature Block** below, OR sign electronically
   via DocuSign / similar (the project will accept either).
3. Fill in **Schedule A** with all Designated Employees, including
   their full names, GitHub usernames, and corporate email addresses.
4. Email the signed PDF to `legal@openlarm.org` (placeholder; until
   that mailbox is live, open a tracking issue and the maintainers
   will provide the current submission address).
5. Wait for confirmation. The Project will:
   - Add Your company to a public `CCLA-signers.md` file
     (company name only; employee list is kept private).
   - Notify You by email when the signing is on file.

## Frequently asked

**Q. Can a small company / startup sign?**
A. Yes. The Agreement is the same regardless of company size.

**Q. We already have a CLA with another OSS project. Can we reuse it?**
A. Probably not directly. Each project's CLA is a separate contract.
However, if your existing CLA is the Apache CCLA v2.0, the substantive
terms are very similar; ask the Project to confirm in writing.

**Q. We can't accept Apache 2.0 internally. Can we negotiate?**
A. The Project does not currently offer alternative licences. Please
contact the maintainers to discuss possible commercial-licence
arrangements.

**Q. What if an employee leaves?**
A. Send a written supplement to remove them from Schedule A. Their
prior Contributions remain licensed under the Agreement (Section 6).

**Q. Are individual contributors who happen to work at our company
also covered?**
A. Only if they are listed as Designated Employees. Employees who
contribute on their own time, in a personal capacity, with no use of
company resources, may sign as individual contributors via DCO instead.
The Project encourages employees to clarify which capacity they are
contributing in.
