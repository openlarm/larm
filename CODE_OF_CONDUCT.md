# Code of Conduct

The LARM project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as
its Code of Conduct. The canonical text applies in full and is
incorporated here by reference:

- Canonical URL: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
- Plain text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.txt

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub
repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, RFCs, any chat or
forum we operate, and in-person events that represent the project —
and also applies when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

---

## Scope clarifications specific to LARM

In addition to the canonical Covenant, the following apply in LARM's
spaces:

1. **Safety-critical discussions stay professional.** Debates about
   risk-model parameters, hard-stop thresholds, or the Safety Notice
   can get heated. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, bad
   faith assumptions, and dismissive language are not.
2. **Regional calibration disputes are technical disputes.** LARM is
   used across jurisdictions. Disagreements over a region adapter's
   calibration, regulatory interpretation, or operator practice are
   technical matters. Keep them technical.
3. **Operational-incident context is handled with care.** If a
   contributor shares details of a real drone incident to motivate a
   change, treat that information with the discretion they would
   expect. Do not dox operators, pilots, or organisations mentioned in
   incident reports.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation of the Code of Conduct:

- **Preferred:** email `conduct@openlarm.org`. Until the `openlarm.org`
  domain is live, contact the repo owner `@mkkb2156` privately via
  GitHub direct message or at `teddyduann@gmail.com`.
- **Alternative:** use the GitHub report-abuse flow on the relevant
  issue, PR, or comment.

Reports are read only by the committee members listed in
`MAINTAINERS.md` under "Code of Conduct response". If the person you
would normally report to is the subject of the report, escalate to any
other committee member listed there.

All reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible. We
will tell you what action — if any — we have taken, unless you
specifically ask us not to follow up with you.

---

## Enforcement

The response team follows the four-tier enforcement ladder described
in the canonical Contributor Covenant 2.1 (Correction → Warning →
Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban). Decisions are made by a majority of
the response team; the reported person recuses themselves if they are
on the team.

Appeals of enforcement actions go to any committee member not
involved in the original decision, within 30 days of the action.
